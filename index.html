Go-to-Market Structure
关键 — Channel Partner
Find a local BD partner — ideally someone from a tier-1 sell-side with buy-side relationships. This is not optional for mainland penetration. Cold outreach conversion: <5%. Warm intro via shared contact: 30–50%.
Pilot Structure
Offer 60–90 day paid pilots to reduce commitment risk. Pilot pricing at 20–30% of full contract. Evidence-based renewal framing with measurable performance metrics.
Pricing Localization
Mainland pricing: 30–50% of Western equivalents at initial rollout. HK pricing can mirror international rates. Wind terminal benchmark: ¥30K–80K/yr per seat — Parallax needs to price relative to this floor.
Content as BD
Run sector-specific Parallax reports (e.g. China consumer, EV supply chain) as lead generation. Send to named targets with a specific observation about their public investment thesis. Gives a concrete conversation hook vs. generic pitch.

Market Barriers

Five structural barriers to entry, each with a mitigation path. Understanding these shapes both BD strategy and product positioning.

Critical · Legal
Data Localization & Cross-Border Rules
China's Cybersecurity Law + Data Security Law + PIPL create strict cross-border data transfer requirements. Investment data may qualify as "important data" requiring CSRC/CAC review. Parallax's cloud architecture must support data residency for mainland clients.

Mitigation: HK-hosted access for Wave 1. Mainland data residency planning for Wave 3.
Critical · Regulatory
ICP License & AI Governance
Any SaaS platform serving mainland users needs ICP filing (工信部). AI-generated investment analysis may require CSRC sign-off post-2024 AI governance standards for fund management.

Mitigation: HK-hosted access sidesteps mainland ICP as interim strategy. Structure mainland offering under local partner entity to absorb regulatory burden.
High · Cultural
关系 (Guanxi) Dynamics
Chinese institutional procurement is relationship-first. Cold outreach conversion is <5%. A warm intro from a shared contact is worth six months of email campaigns.

Mitigation: Invest in a local BD partner with existing institutional relationships before launching formal outreach. Conference circuit (APB Summit, Caproasia) is the warm-meeting factory.
High · Competitive
Wind's Switching Cost Moat
Wind is embedded in every major institution's research workflow. Analysts cannot imagine working without it. Attempting to replace Wind triggers immediate rejection.

Mitigation: Position as additive, never competitive. "Wind + Parallax" framing. Emphasize different workflow step: data retrieval (Wind) vs. investment judgment (Parallax).
Medium · Pricing
Price Anchoring to Domestic Tools
Mainland institutions benchmark against Wind (¥30K–80K/yr per seat). International SaaS pricing feels expensive by comparison. HK/FO segment is less price-sensitive.

Mitigation: Localized pricing tiers essential. ROI framing ("alpha generated per dollar spent") beats cost comparison. Start with HK pricing, establish track record, then negotiate mainland pricing from a position of proof.
